# CareMatrix: Healthcare Analytics Dashboard

A 2-page Power BI dashboard that shows who the patients are, how many are admitted, what care costs, and which hospitals, doctors, conditions and medications drive results.

![CareMatrix Dashboard](Care%20Matrix%20Combined.jpeg)

## Pages
- **Healthcare Overview:** patients, admissions, billing, length of stay, patient journey, gender and age mix, blood type by condition
- **Hospital Performance:** top hospitals and doctors by patients, billing and recovery rate by condition, medication use

## Key insights
- 40K patients and 54.966K admissions, with $1.4bn in total billing
- Average length of stay is 15.5 days and average patient age is 51.5
- Cancer has the highest recovery rate (50%), and obesity has the lowest (27%)
- Billing is almost even across conditions, between $0.23bn and $0.24bn each

## Tools
Power BI

## Filters
Gender, medical condition, and age category
